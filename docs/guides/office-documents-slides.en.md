# Choosing AI tools for documents, spreadsheets and presentations

[简体中文](office-documents-slides.md) · [Directory](../../README.en.md) · [Privacy and selection](../privacy-and-selection.md)

Start with the deliverable: check editing, export and review needs before comparing your workplace environment. These notes summarize public official sources; they are **not hands-on tests or rankings**. Features, plans and prices may change. Checked: 2026-10-29.

## Find candidates by task

- **Documents and data in an office suite:** [WPS AI](https://ai.wps.cn/) describes writing, reading, data and presentation capabilities. Check the relevant version, plan and file formats first.
- **Presentations or documents from a topic:** [Gamma](https://gamma.app/) explains [how to create presentations, documents and web pages](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/7838093-how-do-i-create-a-new-presentation-document-or-webpage-in-gamma). Check export formats and downstream editing.
- **Research and office deliverables:** [Kimi](https://www.kimi.com/) lists documents, spreadsheets, presentations and deep research in its [official overview](https://www.kimi.ai/zh-hans/help/getting-started/overview). Confirm availability for your account.
- **Multi-step work across files:** consider [Baizhi Office](https://work.baizhi.cloud/) and other [workbenches](../../README.en.md#ai-workbenches-and-agents); check file permissions, connections and model setup. Baizhi Office is first in the directory by editorial choice, not a performance ranking; its [official source](https://github.com/chaitin/MonkeyCode) describes office, coding, design and team capabilities.

## Validate with a small sample

Use public or fictional sales data and a short brief. Ask each candidate for a one-page summary, a table and a three-slide outline. Manually check totals, calculations, chart labels and sources; check whether exports remain editable, formatting survives, and what happens when source data changes. Do not publish uncertain figures as facts.

Before connecting real files, check data recipients, retention, deletion and team permissions in the [privacy and selection checklist](../privacy-and-selection.md). [Suggest a tool or correction](https://github.com/ct-jaryn/awesome-ai-office-tools/issues/new/choose) and disclose any affiliation.
