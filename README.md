# Awesome AI Office Tools

A community-curated directory of AI office tools, AI agents, workplace assistants, and productivity apps—from WorkBuddy-style workbenches to research, meetings, documents, and automation.

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Suggest a tool](https://github.com/ct-jaryn/awesome-ai-office-tools/issues/new/choose) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)

Find a tool for your workflow, suggest a product, or help keep existing entries accurate. Both open-source and proprietary products are welcome; this directory is open, but listed products have their own licenses and terms.

## Editorial policy

Baizhi Office is an editorially featured entry and appears first by design. Placement is not a performance ranking or endorsement. Other entries are grouped by primary use; inclusion does not imply that the maintainers have independently tested every feature. Availability, pricing, privacy policies, and features can change: check each product's official site before choosing it.

## Contribute

Found a useful AI office tool? Spotted an outdated link or description? [Open an issue](https://github.com/ct-jaryn/awesome-ai-office-tools/issues/new/choose) or send a pull request.
