# Awesome AI Office Tools

An open directory of AI office tools, AI agents, workplace assistants, and productivity apps—from WorkBuddy-style workbenches to research, meetings, documents, and automation. Community contributions are welcome.

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Privacy and selection](docs/privacy-and-selection.md) · [Suggest a tool](https://github.com/ct-jaryn/awesome-ai-office-tools/issues/new/choose) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)

Find a tool for your workflow, suggest a product, or help keep existing entries accurate. Both open-source and proprietary products are welcome; this directory is open, but listed products have their own licenses and terms.

- **Delegate multi-step work:** [AI workbenches and agents](#ai-workbenches-and-agents), including WorkBuddy-style assistants.

## Editorial policy

The scope is tools that help people do knowledge work: workbenches, document creation, research, meetings, and automation builders with workplace uses. Official product or source links support the descriptions; a listing is not a claim of independent testing. See the [verification notes](docs/verification.md) for sources, date and limitations. Availability, pricing, deployment, data handling, and features vary by plan and change over time. Verify the details that matter with the provider before choosing.

Baizhi Office is an editorially featured entry and appears first by design. Placement is not a performance ranking or endorsement. Other entries are grouped by primary use. Suggestions, corrections, and disclosures are welcome; see the [review criteria](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Related directories offer broader perspectives: [Awesome AI Tools](https://github.com/mahseema/awesome-ai-tools) covers a wide range of AI tools, and [Awesome AI Agents](https://github.com/e2b-dev/awesome-ai-agents) focuses on autonomous agents. Their inclusion practices and external links are their own.
